The Moral Culpability Cube

Agency · Cause · Relationship

Not all wrongs weigh the same. The same act can sit anywhere from a passing failure to a grave betrayal. How much it weighs depends on what you intended, what you did, and who you owed.

Answer three honest questions about something on your conscience. The cube will show you where it falls among 64 positions of moral weight, and what it would take to do better.

Agency
Your will: planned, impulsive, careless, or accidental
Cause
Your hand: did it, enabled it, allowed it, or missed it
Relationship
Your bond: sworn duty, family, neighbor, or stranger
